## Rates
A [[Rates|rate]] is something that changes with another. For example, when we drive on the road the speed-limit sign prompts us to drive at a specific *rate* like 60 miles *per* hour. We are driving 60 miles every hour at that *rate*. In math, we say that we are driving 60 miles *with respect* to one hour. So you can think of "with respect" to be the same thing as the "per" in miles per hour.
Rates do not need to be exclusive to time. Another driving metaphor to explain this is fuel efficiency. We describe how far our car can drive before going to the gas station in miles *per* gallon. Using math lingo, that's miles *with respect* to one gallon.
